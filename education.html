Warehouse Inventory Monitoring System with Barcode Scanner and SMS Notification

BSIT Capstone  ·  STI College San Pablo  ·  December 2022  ·  Client: Paring's Pharmacy

A desktop inventory monitoring system built for Paring’s Pharmacy and delivered to the client in December 2022. Not a demo — an actual working system used by a real business.

  • Built product record management, stock level monitoring, expiration date tracking, and a real-time warehouse status dashboard for pharmacy staff to use daily.
  • Integrated a barcode scanner so products could be looked up instantly by scanning, and an SMS notification system that automatically alerts staff when stock is low or expiration dates are approaching.
  • Delivered by a four-person team — covered requirements gathering, system design, development, testing, and final client presentation.
