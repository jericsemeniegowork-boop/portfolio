How Each Role Connects to QA

ServiceNow ticket handling → defect lifecycle discipline

Handling 10 tickets a day in ServiceNow at Accenture built the habit of writing complete, clear notes on every issue — the same discipline that makes a defect report useful in any QA role.

SAP process validation → end-to-end thinking

Testing SAP S/4HANA workflows showed how business processes can fail in ways only visible in the database — which is why SQL checks were always part of validation, not just screen clicks.

UAT support → user perspective

UAT work at Accenture was less about clicking through screens and more about confirming business users could complete their real tasks without hitting errors or needing workarounds.

TCL & freelance → documentation foundation

Documenting 30 issues a day at TCL and then continuing through freelance work built the habit of treating every test result as a record — clear enough that someone else can pick up where the work left off.
